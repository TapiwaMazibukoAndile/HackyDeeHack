HackyDeeHack concept

One grocery app. Delivery, or a faster walk through the store.

A mock 60-minute delivery app with an In store mode that maps products to the shelves of three demo store layouts.

  1. Add a few things to your cart.
  2. In the cart, choose Plan my route.
  3. Pick a store, then tap Track my steps on a phone, or Simulate walking.

“ASAP-ish!” is a made-up brand for this prototype. Prices and stores are sample data, and no orders are placed.
